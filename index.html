HOW HYDROVERSE WORKS

Real-time 3D
hydrodynamic simulation.

    Map the ground & riverbed

  2. 02
    Solve 2D Shallow Water Equations

    Kurganov-Petrova hydrodynamic solver computed in real-time on WebGL2 framebuffers with strict mass conservation.

    Simulate & deploy defenses

    Draw 2.8m quay walls or sandbag lines live, evaluate building danger levels (Yellow/Red), and compare protected vs baseline flood telemetry side-by-side.
